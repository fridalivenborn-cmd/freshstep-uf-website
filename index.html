Discover the product

Used shoes shouldn't ruin the rest of your bag.

Training shoes collect dirt, moisture and unpleasant odors. When placed directly in a gym bag or backpack, they can spread to your other belongings.

Dirt

Soil and particles from training shoes spread to your belongings.

Moisture

Dampness from sweaty shoes creates humidity in your bag.

Odors

Unpleasant smells spread throughout your bag and belongings.

Meet FreshStep.

FreshStep is a reusable shoe bag designed specifically for storing and transporting used shoes. Keep your gym bag, backpack and car clean while protecting your shoes.

How It Works

1

Put your shoes inside

Place your used training shoes inside the FreshStep bag.

2

Add the activated carbon

Remove the two activated carbon bags from their elastic holders and place them inside the bag together with the shoes.

3

Zip and go

Close the zipper and carry your shoes separately from the rest of your belongings.

Activated carbon helps reduce unpleasant odors.

The activated carbon bags work by adsorbing odor molecules from your shoes, helping to keep your bag fresh. Remove the bags from their elastic holders and place them inside with your shoes for maximum effectiveness.

Silica gel absorbs moisture.

The two silica gel bags are permanently attached inside the FreshStep bag. They continuously work to absorb moisture from your shoes, keeping the bag dry and preventing mold and mildew growth.

About FreshStep UF

FreshStep UF is a student-run company from Per Brahegymnasiet in Jönköping, Sweden.

We are four students who wanted to create a simple and practical solution to a common problem for people who regularly use training shoes.

Our goal is to combine functionality, design and sustainability in one product.
